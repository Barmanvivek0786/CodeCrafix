About Codecrafix

Driven by Code, Powered by Community

Founded by Vivek Barman, Codecrafix is a progressive technology studio based in Satna, MP, specializing in electrical-to-digital transformations, real-world app architectures, and interactive game engineering. We document our entire build process transparently.

  • Electrical-to-digital transformations — turning physical, real-world problems into reliable software products.
  • Real-world app architectures — engineered to scale, maintain and ship without breaking.
  • Transparent build process — every decision, commit and release documented in public.
